### 🟢 核心 Prompt 結構（通用範本）

你可以將以下結構組合起來，形成一個完整的 Prompt：

> **[整體風格描述] + [頁面主要標題] + [左側主表格詳述] + [右側/下方功能區塊詳述] + [插畫與裝飾細節]**

---

### 🔵 詳細 Prompts 拆解與範例

#### 1. 整體風格與色調 (Style & Color Palette)

這是決定畫面第一眼感覺的關鍵。

- **Prompt 關鍵字：**
    
    - **風格：** Kawaii planner page (可愛手帳頁面), Japanese-style cute illustration (日系可愛插畫), clean bullet journal layout (整潔的子彈筆記版面), vector illustration (向量插畫), soft hand-drawn texture (柔軟的手繪質感).
        
    - **色調：** Soft pastel color palette (柔和的粉彩色調), cream-colored background (米色/奶油色背景), warm light colors (溫暖的亮色系).
        
    - **光線：** Soft, even lighting (柔和均勻的光線).
        

#### 2. 頂部標題與日期區 (Top Header & Date)

- **Prompt 關鍵字：**
    
    - At the top, a prominent stylized title in cute Chinese calligraphy: "[你的標題，例如：考試準備追蹤表]".
        
    - A small calendar box on the top left: "2026年 [月份]月" with a heart or star icon.
        
    - A motivational quote banner below the title: "[你的金句，例如：加油！未來的你一定會感謝現在努力的自己]".
        

#### 3. 左側主表格區 (Main Tracking Table - Left Side)

這是打卡表的核心。

- **Prompt 關鍵字：**
    
    - A large grid table for the whole month (e.g., 31 rows for 5/1 to 5/31).
        
    - **欄位標題 (Table Headers)：** Headers include "日期" (Date), "星期" (Day of week), and specific trackable items (e.g., "國文", "英文", "數學", "存50", "存100", "重訓", "有氧").
        
    - **圖示 (Icons)：** Each item header has a cute small icon above the text (e.g., a book for "國文", a coin for "存錢", a dumbbell for "重訓").
        
    - **打卡格 (Checkboxes)：** Columns for "日期" and "星期" show the date/day (e.g., "5/1 五", "5/2 六"). The tracking columns have cute empty circles (or check-marks/stars) for daily tracking.
        

#### 4. 功能性區塊 (Functional Sections - Right/Bottom)

這些區塊讓打卡表更豐富。你需要列出要包含哪些區塊：

- **「本週小總結」 (Weekly Summary)：**
    
    - A section titled "本週小總結" on the right.
        
    - Rows for each week (e.g., 第1週, 第2週), each with date ranges and blank lines for notes/amounts.
        
- **「給自己的一句話」 (Note to Self)：**
    
    - A framed text box titled "給自己的一句話".
        
    - Maybe with a small cute animal (like a rabbit or bear) writing next to it.
        
- **「成就徽章區」 (Achievement Badges)：**
    
    - A section titled "成就徽章區" at the bottom.
        
    - Several circular dashed placeholders with a title below each (e.g., "連續7天完成", "全勤完成", "專注學霸").
        
    - Maybe include 2-3 colorful badges already "earned" (as seen in image_0 and image_1).
        
- **「心情貼紙區」 (Mood Stickers)：**
    
    - A section titled "心情貼紙區".
        
    - A grid of cute face emoji stickers (happy, neutral, sad, etc.) to choose from.
        
- **「KPI 進度條」 (KPI Progress Bar)：**
    
    - A section titled "KPI 進度條".
        
    - A stylized progress bar with labels (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) and a small flag at the end.
        
    - A blank space to write the goal (e.g., "本月讀書目標: ____ 小時").
        

#### 5. 插畫與裝飾元素 (Illustrations & Decorations)

這些細節能增加「可愛感」。

- **Prompt 關鍵字：**
    
    - **主插畫：** A central cute character illustration. E.g., for study: a girl studying at a desk with books (image_0); for saving: a piggy bank with coins (image_1); for exercise: chibi characters exercising (image_2).
        
    - **裝飾性小插畫：** Sprinkle cute small icons and characters throughout the page (little bears, rabbits, stars, hearts, flowers, leaves, pushpins, washi tape).
        
    - **邊框與背景：** The whole composition is framed by a soft border on a clean cream background. The layout is structured but feels warm and hand-drawn.

### 💡 進階技巧：如何「替換主題」

只需更換核心關鍵字即可：

1. **更改主題：** 將所有 "考試/Study" 相關的詞（如 "國文", 書本 icon）替換為新的主題（如 "存錢/Saving", 硬幣 icon）。
    
2. **更改欄位：** 在主表格中，更換表格標題和對應的小圖示。
    
3. **更改主插畫：** 將書桌女孩換成存錢筒（如 image_1）或運動人物（如 image_2）。
    
4. **更改功能區：** 存錢表可能需要 "本月目標金額"（image_1），運動表可能需要 "心率/睡眠"（image_2）。
