
Basic Markdown commands supported by Obsidian include:

|Syntax|Description|
|---|---|
|`#` + space|Level 1 heading|
|`##` + space|Level 2 heading|
|`****` (four asterisks)|Bold text|
|`~~~~` (four tildes)|Strikethrough (deleted text)|
|`====` (four equals)|Highlight text|
|`````` (six backticks)|Code block|
|`>`|Blockquote|
|`-` + space|Unordered list|
|`1.` + space|Ordered list|
|Right-click menu|Insert tables, dividers, math blocks|
